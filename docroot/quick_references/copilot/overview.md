BV-BRC Copilot is a chat assistant for BV-BRC. Ask in plain language and it answers questions about BV-BRC, searches BV-BRC data, looks through your workspace, prepares and submits analysis jobs, builds genome and feature groups, and helps you read job results.

**Copilot is in beta and can make mistakes.** Nothing that changes your account runs until you answer **Yes**: in Plan mode (the default) Copilot prepares jobs and groups and asks you first. Check what it prepared before you agree.

Results of jobs Copilot submits, and files you attach, are saved in your workspace under `home/chats/<chat id>/`. Use **Open Folder** in a chat's cog menu to get there, and **View Jobs** to see that chat's jobs.

Full guide: [BV-BRC Copilot documentation](https://www.bv-brc.org/docs/quick_references/copilot/about.html)
